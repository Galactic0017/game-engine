How ATOM & Fees Work

  1. Wallet connect (Keplr): stores address, name, heroes, level, leaderboard. No seed phrase, no signing in this build for players.
  2. Level up: waves give XP and credits. Heroes unlock at levels 1, 3, 5, 8, 12, 15.
  3. List: pick a hero you own, set an ATOM price. The fee is shown before anyone pays.
  4. Buy: send seller net + CEO fee in two sends, memo = listing ID, submit both TXIDs.
  5. CEO panel: opens only when the connected Keplr wallet is the CEO address, proved by signing a message with your keys (ADR-36, like Swap: non-custodial, you sign every action). Verify both explorer links, amounts, recipients, memo, then approve. Hero moves.

Fee: default 5%, configurable 4–7%. Example: 10 ATOM price at 5% = 9.5 ATOM to seller + 0.5 ATOM to CEO.

Not real yet: no on-chain verification, no escrow, no real ATOM movement. Real mainnet needs a CosmWasm escrow contract, audit, and legal review.
