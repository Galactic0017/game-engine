How to play — Cosmos BTD

Click a tower, then click the board to place it (not on the path). Press 1–8 to pick towers, Space to start a wave, P to pause, R to restart. Place your equipped hero by clicking the board. 50 waves + endless mode — boss every 10. Speed 1x–3x, auto-start, and targeting (First / Last / Strong / Weak / Closest / Elite) are in the bar above. Protect the ATOM orb at the end of the lane.
